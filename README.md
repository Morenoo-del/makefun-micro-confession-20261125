One micro-confession, written exactly as it was typed and never sent — a To: line naming who it was meant for, then the unsent line itself. No explanation after it; the reason it stayed in drafts is felt in the words.

> To: Dad
>
> i practiced telling you i quit the job you brag about to your friends, then deleted it before your birthday so you could keep the story a little longer

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt`.
